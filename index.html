About Us

Trusted by thousands of customers for over 15 years

We Provide Quality Auto Repairs with Honest Pricing

TOP GEAR AUTOMOTIVE was founded in 2008 with a simple mission: to provide honest, reliable auto repair services at fair prices. Our team of certified technicians has over 100 years of combined experience.

We use only the highest quality parts and the latest diagnostic equipment to ensure your vehicle is repaired correctly the first time. We stand behind our work with a 12-month/12,000-mile warranty on all services.

Whether you need routine maintenance or major repairs, you can trust the experts at TOP GEAR AUTOMOTIVE to keep your vehicle running safely and efficiently.
